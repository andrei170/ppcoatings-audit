How The Google Budget Actually Works — A Correction
You asked whether an unspent daily budget rolls over. We said yes. The accurate answer:
  • You only pay when someone clicks. No clicks, no charge.
  • Google balances spend across the month, not day by day. An unspent day is not banked and carried forward as such — instead, on busier days Google may spend up to twice the daily amount to catch the searches that are there.
  • It will never charge more than your monthly limit — roughly the daily budget multiplied by 30.4. At £1,500 a month, that is about £49 a day on average.
  • Setting it to Northern Ireland only does not make it more expensive. It concentrates the same money on the searches you actually want.
