One Decision Today, Not Two. Start The System. Decide On Advertising In Five Weeks, With Numbers.

You said the two things were a system that makes you follow up, and pay-per-click behind it. We agree — and they should not be bought at the same time.

Track B is £3,000 a month and it should not start until week five, because until the quote list exists there is no way to prove an advert produced anything.

Track A — The Quote System And Estimating Page
GBP3,000 one-off, both together no monthly fee (GBP2,500 + GBP1,500 if taken separately) Starts week one. Built once, yours to run. WHAT YOU GET A list of every job you quote, with a stage on each -- enquiry, survey, quote sent, chasing, won, lost The value on every quote, so you can see what is out at any given moment An automatic chase -- when a quote has sat unanswered for a fortnight, the system tells somebody to ring The estimate page on your existing website -- what are you coating, how big, what condition, when Enquiries land straight on the list, from the website and the phone, with a text alert The back catalogue -- old quotes that never heard back, loaded and worked through Brochure and data-sheet tracking -- which specifier downloaded what Website measurement installed FREE, whatever else you decide Training for your team, and a review at the end of month one
Why this one first, and why it has no monthly fee

Your quote is the product. Everything in this business turns on how many go out and how many come back, and right now nobody can tell you either number. That is not a marketing problem, it is a visibility problem, and it is fixable in weeks.

The back catalogue is the part that usually pays for it. Quotes that went out and never heard anything back are not lost — most of them were simply never chased. Loading them in and working through them is the first thing we do.

No monthly fee means your downside is bounded at three thousand pounds. If after a month the list tells you nothing useful, you have still got the list, the estimate page and the measurement, and you owe nothing further.

Track B — Specification-Led Paid Search, Northern Ireland Only
GBP3,000 a month = GBP1,500 our management + GBP1,500 paid straight to Google as ad spend From week five. Month to month. Stop it whenever you like. A free keyword report FIRST -- what people in Northern Ireland actually search for, before a penny is spent Campaign 1 Specification / survey aimed at the people writing the spec, not the person buying 20 litres Campaign 2 Competitor displacement shown when someone searches a competitor's name Campaign 3 CPD for practices architects and engineers booking a presentation Geo-restricted to Northern Ireland -- nobody in Glasgow or Manchester sees it, because you told us not to sell there Judged on surveys booked and specifications written, not clicks. Anything not producing gets switched off.
Half of that figure is not ours

£1,500 of the £3,000 goes straight to Google as advertising spend. It is not AG1 revenue and we will show you the account. Our fee is the other £1,500.

And we would rather you did not start this today. Until the quote list is running there is no honest way to tell whether an advert produced a survey. Starting both at once means paying for advertising you cannot measure — which is exactly the thing you have been doing without meaning to.

What We Are Deliberately Not Selling You Today

Saying no to a bigger sale on purpose

The trade counter is the largest single thing on your list and we have left it out. Quoting it blind would either overcharge you for a price list or underquote an integration and then come back for more. Neither is a good start.

It belongs after the quote system exists, because by then we will know how your accounts actually work rather than guessing.

How You Would Judge Whether This Worked
  • End of month one: how many quotes went out, what they were worth in total, how many were won, how many are still sitting unanswered. You cannot produce those four numbers today.
  • End of month two: how many of the back-catalogue quotes came back to life once somebody rang them.
  • Week five, the advertising decision: with the quote list running, you can see whether paid search is worth £3,000 a month. If the numbers do not support it, do not start it.
  • Throughout: surveys booked and specifications written. Not clicks, not impressions, not “reach”.
