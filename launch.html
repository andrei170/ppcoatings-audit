Three Things To Sort Before Monday, And Two Decisions To Make In The Room.

For Warren and Davey · 30 September 2026 · AG1 Consulting

Nothing here takes long, but two of them decide how much of Monday is useful.

I am building the system between now and then. What I cannot do on my own is get your old quotes out of OrderWise, or decide what your sales stages should be called.

If the first three arrive before Monday, you walk into a system with your own customers already in it rather than an empty one.

Three things to send • Two decisions on the day • Warren is away Thursday and Friday
1. The Old Quotes, Out Of OrderWise

This is the one that matters most. Every quote you have issued and never heard back on is work you have already done the estimating for. Getting that in front of you is the fastest thing the system does, and it is the only part I cannot build without you.

WHAT TO EXPORT Every quote issued in the last 24 months, whatever the outcome. Won, lost, and the ones that just went quiet. FIELDS, IF OrderWise WILL GIVE THEM Company name Value of the quote Contact name Date issued Email address Outcome, if recorded Phone number Who issued it Site or address Product or system quoted FORMAT CSV or Excel. One row per quote. Do not worry about tidying it. Messy is fine, I will sort it. WHO Probably your IT chap, or whoever gets on best with OrderWise.
If OrderWise will not give you all of that, send what it will

Company name, a contact, a phone number and a value is enough to start. Everything else is a bonus.

And if there is a separate list of your steel fabricator customers, the one Davey mentioned that has been built up over the years, send that too. It is probably the most valuable thing in the building.

2. Which Address Belongs To Your Accounts Man
YOU SENT ME SIX wjp@ djp@ sos@ jko@ mjp@ info@ WHAT I NEED TO KNOW Which one is your accounts chap's own address?
Why I am asking rather than just using info@

info@ is a shared mailbox. If two people are signed in as it, every note and every follow-up looks like it came from the same person, and you lose exactly the accountability Davey said he wanted.

He should have a login in his own name, with info@ still feeding into the system as the address the website points at. Both things at once, which is what you actually want.

3. Your Legal And Trading Name

Warren has sent this, so it is done. Noted here only so the list is complete.

Decision One — What Your Sales Stages Are Called

Every quote will sit in a stage so you can see at a glance where everything is. I could pick these myself, but they would be my words rather than yours, and people do not use a system that talks about their job in a language they do not use. Four minutes on Monday.

Option A — the short one
1. Enquiry 2. Quote issued 3. Chasing 4. Won 5. Lost

Five stages, nothing to think about. Best if the main thing you want is to stop quotes going quiet without anybody noticing.

Option B — the one that shows the specification work
1. Enquiry 2. Survey or specification 3. Quote issued 4. Chasing 5. Won 6. Lost

Six stages, one extra step. Best if you want to see how much of your work involves writing a specification before a price, because that is the higher margin end and at the moment nothing counts it.

Change the words to whatever you actually say

If you call it a site visit rather than a survey, we call it a site visit. If there is a stage in between that I have missed, tell me and it goes in.

Decision Two — When A Quote Counts As Gone Quiet
THE SYSTEM CAN NUDGE SOMEBODY TO RING A CUSTOMER WHEN A QUOTE HAS SAT WITH NO REPLY. How many days should that be? 7 days probably too keen for your bigger jobs 14 days a reasonable starting point 21 days 30 days for the very large ones It can also be different by value. Small jobs chased sooner, big ones left longer.
Warren already half answered this

You said the big jobs take a while and you would rather judge properly at around ninety days. So a seven day nudge on a large specification job would be annoying and would get switched off within a fortnight.

Start longer and shorten it later. It is a number in a box, not a rebuild.

What The Quote Record Will Hold

This is what turns a list of names into something Davey can actually search. Tell me on Monday what is wrong or missing.

ON EVERY QUOTE Company and contact Value Phone and email Date issued Site or address Who owns it Product or system quoted Stage Colour or RAL reference Area, in square metres Quantity Substrate (steel, concrete, roof, floor) When they want it done SO YOU CAN ASK THINGS LIKE "Every steel fabricator quote over five thousand from the last year that we never heard back on."
