What You Said You Actually Want
"A system that systemises it and makes us follow up and act upon it, and then supported by pay-per-click advertising. Essentially, that's the two things." "I don't need eureka, I just need something that's going to improve where we are." "Bigger doesn't always mean that we have to handle it."
Why we have not come back with a transformation

You asked for two specific things and told us plainly you are not after a revolution. So this is two pieces of work, priced separately, in the order you put them in — the system first, advertising after, and only if the system shows it is worth it.

“Bigger doesn’t always mean that we have to handle it” is the sentence that shaped the advertising half. We are not trying to bring you more enquiries. We are trying to bring you the right ones.

The storage constraint is the most important thing you told us

Most suppliers would hear “we want more work” and sell you more enquiries. You told us the limit is physical space and that a new building is being built. So more volume is the wrong instrument until that building is up.

That is why the plan goes after fewer, larger, specified jobs rather than more of everything.
