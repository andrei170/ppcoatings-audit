What this model does not do

It does not predict that you will recover any quote, win any job or earn any pound. It takes figures you supply and shows what would have to be true to close the gap you set. The recovery rate and the margin shift are assumptions you choose, not rates we have measured for your business — because nothing in your business currently records them. That is precisely the problem the plan is built to fix.

The counter-time figure is shown as hours returned, deliberately not converted into money. What an extra estimating hour is worth depends on your margins and your close rate, and we are not going to invent that number.

These are the four numbers we do not have.

Average delivery value, quotes issued, quotes won, and counter time. With those, this stops being a model and starts being your business.
